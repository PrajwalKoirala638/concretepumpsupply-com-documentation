# 🧪 SDS vs. MSDS

## What is an SDS?

**SDS** stands for **Safety Data Sheet**.

An SDS communicates important information about a product or chemical, including potential hazards, safe handling, storage, emergency procedures, protective equipment, and other safety considerations.

## What is an MSDS?

**MSDS** stands for **Material Safety Data Sheet**.

MSDS is an older term that was commonly used for safety documentation before the modern SDS format became standard in many jurisdictions.

You may therefore encounter both terms when searching for safety documentation.

### In short:

**MSDS → Older terminology**

**SDS → Modern terminology**

# ⚠️ Important Safety Notice

**Please read the applicable SDS before handling a product or material.**

SDS/MSDS documents are safety resources and may contain important information about hazards, protective equipment, handling, storage, emergency response, transportation, and disposal.

Do not rely solely on an old, incomplete, unofficial, or potentially outdated copy when making safety-critical decisions.

Always consider:

* The current manufacturer's documentation
* Product labeling
* Workplace safety procedures
* Applicable laws and regulations
* Site-specific requirements
* Professional safety guidance

### Most importantly:

**If the current manufacturer-issued SDS differs from a document in this repository, use the current manufacturer-issued documentation and verify the discrepancy.**

This repository is intended to improve access to documentation. It is **not a substitute for professional safety advice, workplace procedures, product labels, or the manufacturer's current documentation.**
